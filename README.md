# Wollaston Spinal Health – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Wollaston Spinal Health is a multidisciplinary spinal health clinic based in Stourbridge, West Midlands, offering chiropractic care, IDD spinal decompression, sports therapy and massage.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning the clinic as a specialist destination for spinal and musculoskeletal care. It brings the clinic's treatment expertise, services and new patient journey into a clearer, more structured experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer and More Distinctive Homepage Messaging
Replaced generic, repetitive messaging with a stronger presentation of the clinic's multidisciplinary approach, specialist spinal treatments and range of expertise. The new messaging communicates what distinguishes Wollaston Spinal Health and helps visitors understand its core offering immediately.

### 2. More Structured Service Discovery
Reorganised the presentation of chiropractic care, IDD spinal decompression, sports therapy, massage and related treatments into clearer service sections. This makes the available treatments easier to explore and helps visitors identify relevant care options without navigating between multiple pages.

### 3. A More Connected New Patient Journey
Brought new patient consultation information, initial assessment expectations, free virtual consultations and appointment enquiry opportunities into a more connected homepage journey. This gives prospective patients a clearer understanding of what happens next and makes arranging an initial consultation more straightforward.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Introduced a clearer content structure to prioritise the clinic's specialist positioning, key treatments and primary conversion opportunities.
- **Layout and Presentation:** Improved the organisation of homepage sections to create a more coherent progression from clinic introduction to services and patient enquiries.
- **Service Visibility:** Gave individual treatment categories clearer presentation, making the clinic's multidisciplinary offering easier to understand.
- **User Experience:** Connected relevant patient information with consultation and enquiry opportunities to reduce unnecessary navigation.
- **Design Consistency:** Established a more consistent visual presentation across typography, spacing, content sections and interactive elements.
- **Conversion Focus:** Strengthened the visibility of new patient consultation enquiries, telephone contact and virtual consultation options.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage creates a clearer introduction to Wollaston Spinal Health, communicates its specialist treatment offering more effectively and provides prospective patients with a more straightforward route towards their first consultation.
